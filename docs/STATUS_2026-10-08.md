# Sunnyfolk Roblox: status update for review

**Date:** 8 October 2026
**Purpose:** an up-to-date account of what's built, what changed since the external review brief of the same day (`Sunnyfolk_External_Review_Brief_2026-10-08.md`), and the decisions still open. Please review it alongside that brief.

**Project:** a Roblox version of Sunnyfolk, a cosy animal town for children aged about 5 to 9. Players pick an animal, meet the residents, collect coins, dress up, adopt a pet, make a home and grow a garden. It's kind, non-competitive and has no fighting. Optional revenue must never undermine trust.

**Source:** GitHub `lschandler81/sunnyfolk-roblox`, branch `claude/town-square-v1`.
- **The code:** Luau, synced into Studio with Rojo: 42 scripts, about 10,700 lines, excluding the generated mesh layout.
- **History:** 41 commits, one per build step plus small fixes.
- **Live game:** owned by the Roblox account Occha1981 (place 121398671118234), and playable on phone, tablet, computer, Xbox and PlayStation.

**A note on the earlier brief.** It was written from a saved `.rbxl` copy that was older than the code. Rojo updates Studio, but the `.rbxl` file only changes when the place is saved or published. So two of its findings were about that old copy rather than the current game; see "Since the external review" below.

## What's built (Steps 1 to 14b of the build plan)

Every step has been tested by the owner in Roblox Studio before being committed: on desktop, in the phone and tablet emulators, with a controller, and with several players where that mattered. Each step was also checked by a separate code-reviewer agent against the project's rules.

### Foundations
- **Safe saving** (Step 2): one DataStore save per player, locked to one server at a time.
  - Every save is stamped, and an older save is never written over a newer one.
  - A version number upgrades old saves one step at a time; the save is now version 7.
  - A player whose save can't be opened is kindly asked to come back, rather than playing unsaved.
  - Every field is tidied when loaded: unknown values are dropped, and numbers are checked for "not a number".
- **The server decides** (Step 3): coins, purchases, homes, pets, goals and the garden are all checked and changed on the server. Every remote is rate-limited and checks the type and range of what it's sent.
- **Analytics** (Step 4):
  - an onboarding funnel (joined, chose an animal, first coin, first hat, first home);
  - coin economy events;
  - a "DailyPresent" funnel and a custom event per finished request (Step 10).
- **Performance** (Steps 5 and 6): the town was measured, then lightened.
  - Lamp lights were removed, and small parts cast no shadows.
  - Nothing listens for touches, and walk-through parts skip raycasts.
  - Animals far from the camera animate less often.
  - Streaming is on.

### Every device
- **Phones and tablets** (Step 7, plus later passes):
  - every screen fits a landscape phone and grows on bigger screens;
  - every touch target is at least 44 pixels;
  - buttons keep clear of the thumbstick and the jump and Run buttons;
  - the HUD sits in one row in the top right.
- **Controllers and consoles** (Step 7b):
  - every screen works with a controller: a yellow ring on the buttons, B to go back, and button pictures for Xbox or PlayStation;
  - everything is bigger on a TV and stays inside the TV-safe area;
  - Roblox's chat window is off on console, as Roblox's console guidelines require.

### Things to do
- **The town square** with its fountain, four shops, a library, a gazebo, cottages and five roads.
- **Three playable animals** (capybara, bunny, guinea pig), each with coats and scarf colours.
- **Three residents** (Clover, Puddles, Pebble) who stroll about, greet you by name and talk in speech bubbles.
- **Coins** that float round the square and are saved between visits.
- **Clover's Hat Shop** (19 hats) and **the Clothes Shop** (Step 11): 13 items in three slots.
  - **Neck:** bandanas, which replace the free scarf.
  - **Body:** aprons, a smock, raincoats and a space suit.
  - **Back:** backpacks.
  - Everything is tried on in a live preview before buying.
- **Homes** (Step 8): 20 plots round two ponds, Home Pond and Lily Pond. A house designer has fixed choices: shape, wall, roof, door, garden, and a sign with the player's Roblox display name.
- **Goals and a guided start** (Step 9): ten goals lead a new player round town, with a yellow arrow at their feet and a pin over the target.
  - The goals are: say hello, pick up coins, meet the residents, look round the Hat Shop, buy a hat, make a home, visit two places, adopt a pet, and pick something you grew.
  - Each pays coins and "sunshine", and sunshine opens gated roads.
  - The finishing bonus is paid only once, even as goals are added.
- **Daily present and request board** (Step 10): a present each day you visit, over seven days, with a hat on day 7. Missing a day doesn't reset the seven. The board has three requests a day, the same for everyone. Neither has timers or pressure wording.
- **Badges** (Step 12): six badges, all created and live:
  - Welcome to Sunnyfolk!
  - Hat Fan
  - Home Sweet Home
  - Coin Collector
  - Seven Sunny Days
  - Sunnyfolk Explorer
- **Pets** (Step 13): adopt a puppy, kitten or duckling for 40 coins.
  - **Names** are picked from a ready-made list; there's no typing.
  - **Changing kind, colour or name** is free after adopting.
  - **Gear:** collars and head things.
  - **It follows you,** sits when you stop, and wags its tail. Everyone sees everyone's pets.
  - **It's cheap to run:** pets are drawn and moved on each player's own screen, with no physics, and were tested with 20 at once with no frame-rate drop.
- **The Garden** (Steps 14 and 14b): Fern's Garden at the end of the Garden road.
  - **Its gate** opens at 6 sunshine.
  - **Everyone has their own six beds** in the same garden: plant (free), water each of three stages, then harvest for coins.
  - **Crops grow by the server's clock while the player is away:**

    | Crop | Each stage | Coins |
    |---|---|---|
    | Sunflowers | 2 min | 4 |
    | Carrots | 10 min | 8 |
    | Strawberries | 30 min | 15 |
    | Giant pumpkin | 3 hours | 30 |

  - **Crops never wither** and can't be taken by anyone else.
  - **The server checks every action,** while each player's own screen draws their crops, so 20 players cost the server almost nothing.
  - **Step 14b polish:** clearer bed signs, a smarter goal pin, compact phone buttons, and a finished well, scarecrow and pet hats.

### Child-safety choices (unchanged, and kept deliberately)
- **No text boxes of our own:** players only talk through Roblox's filtered chat, and pet names come from a fixed list.
- **No combat, no loss state, no random rewards,** no countdowns near anything that costs money, and no pressure wording.
- **No Robux products yet:**
  - nothing is sold;
  - all coins are earned by playing;
  - no place, friendship or story will ever need Robux.

## Where we're at, and what's next

**The current build plan** (`docs/BUILD_STEPS.md`), as written before the review:
- 15 Café shift;
- 16 fishing and the fish book;
- 17 home furniture and interiors;
- 18 emotes and visiting;
- 19 to 23 money: purchase plumbing, three passes, a shop screen and private servers, "Sparkle hats", and optional coin packs;
- 24 to 28: pre-launch, Kids and Select submission, soft launch, a seasonal event, and a club.

**The revision we're considering, following the review:**
1. A short clean-up release: batch the coin analytics, add a message when a home plot moves, check the 20-player setting, and test on real phones and tablets.
2. Garden in onboarding: lower the gate, plant early in the first visit, and a small Garden book.
3. Emotes (wave, cheer, dance, sit), then the Café shift: solo first, better with a friend, picture-based orders, no typing needed.
4. Home Expressions v1: a small personal interior (bed, rug, table, plant, pet bed, shelf, wall decoration, wallpaper and floor), built only while its owner is inside.
5. Fishing and the fish book, using the existing ponds.
6. A soft launch with **no paid products,** reviewing D1 and D7 retention, the onboarding funnel, Garden return and harvest, Café completion, and home placement.
7. Only after that, if the numbers support it: **one cosmetic pack** (a game pass), using the safe receipt pattern already planned.
8. **Cut or parked:**
   - Cut: Double Coins, coin packs, the paid animal pack (extra animals would be earned instead), and capacity perks such as bigger plots or extra pets.
   - Parked until there's evidence: the club coin bonus, Kart Track, private-server pricing, a big map expansion, and season passes.

**How we work:**
- **One build step at a time:** read the step, plan first where marked, write the code, check it builds, then an automatic code review.
- **Testing:** the owner tests in Studio, then it's committed and logged in `PROGRESS.md`.
- **To save credits,** we're moving to a fresh Claude session per step, and a lighter reviewer for routine steps.

## Questions for the reviewer

1. Does the revised order (clean-up, Garden in onboarding, emotes and Café, Home Expressions v1, fishing, soft launch, then one cosmetic pack) look right? Would you move anything?
2. Garden in onboarding: is lowering the gate to 3 sunshine and making "plant a seed" the second or third goal enough, or should the first visit start in the Garden?
3. Garden rewards: is a simple "Garden book" (crops grown, with a sticker for each) a good enough first non-coin reward, or would one placeable garden decoration matter more?
4. Crop times: are 2 min, 10 min, 30 min and 3 hours a good spread for 5 to 9 year olds, or should the slowest be shorter?
5. Homes: is "tell the player when their plot moved" an acceptable stopgap until personal interiors exist?
6. What's the smallest Café shift that would feel worth coming back for?
7. Are there any Roblox Kids or Select requirements, or accessibility needs (for example, how much reading the game expects from 5 to 6 year olds), that this project should handle before soft launch?
